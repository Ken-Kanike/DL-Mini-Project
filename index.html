3. Feature Extraction & Neural Network Architecture

Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCCs) capture the overall spectral timbre and formant structures of instruments and vocals, mapped onto the non-linear human hearing frequency curve.

Shape: (128, 13) • Extracted with librosa.feature.mfcc (n_mfcc=13, hop_length=512)

Chromagram (12 Semitone Pitch Classes) projects spectral energy onto the 12 chromatic musical pitch classes ($C, C\sharp, D, \dots, B$), representing harmonic progressions and chord structures regardless of octave.

Shape: (128, 12) • Extracted with librosa.feature.chroma_stft

Spectral Contrast calculates the decibel difference between spectral peaks and valleys across 7 sub-bands, distinguishing clear melodic instrumentation from percussive or noisy audio.

Shape: (128, 7) • Extracted with librosa.feature.spectral_contrast

Spectral Centroid indicates the center of mass of the audio spectrum, directly correlating with perceived "brightness" or high-frequency dominance in the sound.

Shape: (128, 1) • Extracted with librosa.feature.spectral_centroid
